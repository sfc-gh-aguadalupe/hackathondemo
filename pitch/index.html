Act III — Principles

Four rules, never bent

1 · Vault-first

Agents read before they reason. Live warehouse queries are the last resort, not the reflex.

2 · Permissions per interface

Slack reads. The operator writes. The door decides — not the user, not the prompt.

3 · Eval-gated change

No structural change without behavioral comparison. The gate is deterministic, reproducible, binary.

4 · Local-first, cloud-ready

Everything runs on the SE's machine today. The vault sync is the bridge to scale.

The methodology is the product.
